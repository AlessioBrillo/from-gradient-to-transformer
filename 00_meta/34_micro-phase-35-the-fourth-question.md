## Deep-dive study plan

1. **The regime-selection theory** (the C14 reading): why weight decay ×
   schedule selects solution regimes — Nanda et al., *Progress Measures for
   Grokking via Mechanistic Interpretability* (2023), weight decay as the
   canonical circuit-forming pressure; Gromov, *Grokking: A Memory
   Perspective* (2023); Morwani et al., 2024, the edge-of-numerical-stability
   regime — the dense/sparse boundary; Power et al., 2022 — with Fourier
   sparsity as the order parameter and grokking as a first-order-like
   transition; the phase-diagram vocabulary from statistical mechanics,
   studied as theory before the sweep grid is fixed.
2. **The dense algorithm's computation** (the C13 reading): the DFT of the
   addition table's full-support expansion, per-head Fourier dictionaries
   from MP-29 S3's characterization, QK/OV composition — reading the P=113
   checkpoints as code, not as a "dense blob".
3. **Shortcut learning and universality** (Liu et al., *Transformers Learn
   Shortcuts to Automata*, 2023; Chughtai et al., *A Toy Model of
   Universality*, 2023): what makes a solution a shortcut vs a canonical
   algorithm — the framing C10 hands to C13/C14.
4. **Induction heads at scale** (Olsson et al., *In-Context Learning and
   Induction Heads*, 2022): diag+1 mass, prefix matching, fresh-batches
   dynamics, the d_model ≈ 128 formation floor — the C15 reading if R1
   closes NO-HEAD.
5. **The circuit portrait method** (Olah et al., *Zoom In: An Introduction
   to Circuits*, 2020; Elhage et al., *A Mathematical Framework for
   Transformer Circuits*, 2021; Wang et al., *Interpretability in the Wild*,
   2023): QK/OV decomposition, logit lens, head-role maps — the C16 craft,
   worth learning regardless of the fork.
6. **SAEs where the circuit is dense** (Bricken et al., 2023; Cunningham et
   al., 2024): L0/FVE/dead-features on the dense residual stream — the
   evidence lane shared by C13 and C14, read as evidence once the data
   exists.
7. **Phase diagrams as negative results**: how experimental science reports
   mapped negatives (critical lines, order parameters, falsified
   predictions), and how to present the phase diagram honestly in the paper —
   Gelman & Loken applied, the record's standing countermeasure, fourth pass.

## Strategic tips and architectural best practices

- **The one-question law, fourth execution.** A phase that opens two research
  questions is drift by another name; the unchosen candidates close in the
  same sitting as the choice. The continuum law is the mechanical refusal of
  this drift — proven executable three times, it must simply be executed
  again.
- **The candidate set is frozen before S0, never improvised at it.** C13–C16
  are pre-registered in this file, conditioned on MP-29–MP-34 verdicts; a
  sitting decides, it never invents. The unchosen close with dated reasons
  in the same sitting as the choice.
- **Consumption is execution.** A verdict consumed into an artifact in the
  same sitting is a result; consumed into a paragraph written later it is a
  memory. Row 1 consumes ADR-0008's row-3 verdict in the sitting that owns it.
- **The record teaches, and the receipt compounds.** The third runnable
  artifact is only worth shipping because the first two transcripts proved
  the format; the showcase's story is now "read it, run it, watch me be wrong
  on the record" — three receipts deep.
- **Verdict-agnostic by law.** This roadmap re-plans nothing of MP-29–MP-34;
  its candidates are conditions, not predictions. The fork drill (Ex-H) keeps
  both futures written without pre-committing to either.
- **The shelf is a row, not a mood.** Year three begins with a heartbeat and
  a hostile-webmaster transcript; a live public number that exceeds the
  record is a blocked row, not a to-do.
- **Annexes are amended, never rewritten.** Annex v5 inherits the annex
  doctrine: a new annex appears via a dated fork, the original preserved.
- **The S0 gate is a checklist with receipts.** MP-34's release report
  (ADR-0008 at zero, the live URL, `verify-claims` at 0, the second teaching
  transcript on disk) is a condition with artifacts, not a paragraph — six
  phases stacked without execution would be the permanent warning made real,
  and Session 0 refuses it mechanically.
- **The negative stays the signature.** The row that closes with one reason
  is stamped like the row that launched; the fourth question earns its
  verdict or its scheduled negative with equal polish — and if C14 opens,
  the phase diagram is a *mapped* negative, the strongest form of the

- **The debt row re-verifies; it never re-does.** A stamped closure is

- **Architecture laws unchanged.** `dev` only, GPG-signed, Conventional
