## Deep-dive study plan

1. **The transition as a phase boundary (the C18 reading)**: order
   parameters, critical lines, finite-size scaling — the
   statistical-mechanics vocabulary applied to the dense→memorized boundary;
   Morwani et al., 2024 (the edge-of-numerical-stability regime), Nanda et
   al., *Progress Measures for Grokking via Mechanistic Interpretability*
   (2023), Gromov, *Grokking: A Memory Perspective* (2023), Power et al.,
   2022 — studied as theory before the order-parameter analysis is fixed,
   exactly as the phase diagram's axes were studied before its sweep grid.
2. **The dense algorithm's computation (the C17/C20 reading)**: the DFT of
   the addition table's full-support expansion, per-head Fourier dictionaries
   from the characterization on disk, QK/OV composition — reading the P=113
   checkpoints as code, with the C13/C14 verdicts as the framing.
3. **Shortcut learning and universality** (Liu et al., *Transformers Learn
   Shortcuts to Automata*, 2023; Chughtai et al., *A Toy Model of
   Universality*, 2023): what makes a solution a shortcut vs a canonical
   algorithm — the C10 verdict consumed as framing for whichever regime the
   diagram found.
4. **SAE failure modes where the circuit is dense** (Bricken et al., 2023;
   Cunningham et al., 2024): L0/FVE/dead-features as regime labels, not just
   diagnostics — the C20 reading.
5. **Reproducibility science (the C19 reading, fifth pass on Gelman &
   Loken)**: how a study of receipts is designed — failure-rate baselines,
   per-step pass/fail definitions, and what a reproducibility rate actually
   claims; the record's standing countermeasure applied to its own showcase.
6. **The record teaches, round four**: the fifth question's verdict in four
   registers — the paper's sentence, the annex's sentence, the 30-second
   spoken claim, and the 5-minute teaching explanation with a worked toy a
   stranger can run; the gap between the last two is where my teaching leaks.
7. **Phase diagrams as negative results, deepened**: how a mapped boundary is
   reported honestly — critical lines, order parameters, falsified
   predictions — and how the paper presents a mapped negative as a
   contribution.

## Strategic tips and architectural best practices

- **The one-question law, fifth execution.** A phase that opens two research
  questions is drift by another name; the unchosen candidates close in the
  same sitting as the choice. The continuum law is the mechanical refusal of
  this drift — proven executable four times, it must simply be executed
  again.
- **The candidate set is frozen before S0, never improvised at it.** C17–C20
  are pre-registered in this file, conditioned on MP-29–MP-35 verdicts; a
  sitting decides, it never invents. The unchosen close with dated reasons
  in the same sitting as the choice.
- **Consumption is execution.** A verdict consumed into an artifact in the
  same sitting is a result; consumed into a paragraph written later it is a
  memory. Row 1 consumes ADR-0009's row-3 verdict in the sitting that owns it.
- **The record teaches, and the receipt compounds.** The fourth runnable
  artifact is only worth shipping because the first three transcripts proved
  the format — and if C19 opens, the receipts stop being anecdotes and
  become a measured rate. The showcase's story is now "read it, run it,
  watch me be wrong on the record," four receipts deep.
- **Verdict-agnostic by law.** This roadmap re-plans nothing of MP-29–MP-35;

- **The shelf is a row, not a mood.** Year four begins with a heartbeat and a
