# ADR-0010 — The Fifth Continuum Ledger

## Status

Accepted. Created as the living home of the decision table that
[[00_meta/35_micro-phase-36-the-fifth-question]] (Sessions 0–8) fills: one row
per post-premiere decision, each ending `LAUNCHED (date, verdict)` or
`CLOSED (date, one named reason)`.

## Context

ADR-0009 made the continuum's fourth execution mechanical and MP-35 executes
it now: exactly one new research question opened from the frozen candidate
set C13–C16, the unchosen candidates close with one dated reason each, and
the shelf, the annex, the paper diff, the stranger pipeline and the teaching
lane run as dated rows. ADR-0009 is full and closed at MP-35's release;
closed-then-reopened is a NEW row, never a revision (inherited). What the
program becomes after its fourth executed continuum — the fifth research
question, the fourth runnable teaching artifact, the sixth stranger round,
the shelf's fourth year — is a decision set, and an undecided one is drift by
another name. This ADR makes the fifth execution mechanical under the same
law: exactly one new research question opens per phase, the rest of the
candidate set closes with one dated reason each, and the record's teaching
compounds as a dated row with a runnable receipt, never a mood. The
induction-head fork (C2→C6→C11→C15) is not re-included: its R1 condition has
long since adjudicated, and a fourth re-inclusion would be a mood, not a
candidate.

## Decision

- One table, this file, owned by the vault, filled during Micro-Phase 36's
  sessions and maintained through its release; it is the single source of
  truth the essay annex v6, the paper v6 diff, the site's health page and the
  skill-tree flips cite.
- Row states are strictly: `LAUNCHED` (with date + window + verdict/artifact)
  or `CLOSED` (with date + one named reason). No "awaiting", no "pending", no
  reference without a date.
- The session gate is the same as ADR-0001/0003/0004/0005/0006/0007/0008/0009:
  a session is not over while a row it is responsible for is undated; the
  phase's Session 8 requires zero undated rows.
- **The continuum law, fifth execution**: exactly one new research row
  (row 3) opens per phase, chosen at Session 0 from the pre-registered
  candidate set C17–C20 below; the unchosen candidates close with one dated
  reason each, in the same sitting. Verdict criteria are written into the
  row's cells **before** the window opens and are never edited afterwards;
  the only allowed post-launch edit is "observed".
- ADR-0009's executed rows are consumed here as NEW rows carrying MP-35's
  verdicts — the history is the audit (inherited); nothing is re-negotiated.
- Closed-then-reopened is a NEW row, never a revision (inherited).
- The stranger-review human-dependency rule (ADR-0004) extends to round 6: if
  the feedback intake does not happen within the window, the row closes with
  one reason and the recorded self-review substitute — never a silent skip.
- The standing-debt rule is re-verification, never re-doing: a stamped
  closure is re-checked with its transcript; a genuinely new debt cell is a
  NEW row, never a revision. A pending item cannot outlive a ledger.
- **The pipeline caps at seven**: MP-36 is the last pre-registration until
  the stacked phases execute; the next ledger is written from MP-36's release
  report, not from the habit of pre-registering.

## Consequence

- No post-premiere experiment begins without a dated ledger row and a
  pre-registered protocol: "let's see" is not a row (inherited).
- The record's teaching compounds a fourth time: the teaching lane (row 7)
  ships a fourth public artifact a stranger can run, with the run-transcript
  as its receipt — the showcase's story is "read it, run it, watch me be
  wrong on the record," four receipts deep; and if C19 opens, the receipts
  become a measured rate rather than a mood.
- The capstone ends with a chosen program, not a trailing mood: at any
  release, the ledger states exactly which question is open, which closed with
  a reason, and which surface was maintained — the record compounds one number
  at a time (inherited).
- The next drift front of this repository is defined: a continuum row that
  says "awaiting window" a second phase in a row is drift in the ledger's own
  terms (inherited), and a seventh pre-registration while the stack stands
  unexecuted is the pipeline law's violation — the cap is seven.
