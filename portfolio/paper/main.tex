\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
% Pre-populated from 07_capstone/research-plan.md's "Honest Caveats" --
% expand with anything specific this pass found, do not just copy verbatim
% without checking it still holds.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Micro-scale models: findings may not transfer to larger (7B+) models.
  \item Algorithmic tasks (modular addition, repeated tokens) are cleaner
    than natural language; circuits found here may not be representative
    of circuits in language models.
  \item Attribution patching is a linear approximation with known failure
    modes on large activations (the residual stream); not implemented here
    -- see the skill tree's Research Skills section.
  \item Activation patching can mislead via layernorm denominator effects
    and subspace-projection illusions \citep{makelov2023subspace}.
  \item SAE interpretability is human-judged and features can be illusory
    \citep{makelov2023subspace}.
  \item Single architecture family, single seed distribution (until the
    multi-seed harness covers every rung) -- no claim of universality.
\end{itemize}
